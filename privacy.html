Privacy policy

Session restoration, recent files, PDF export, formulas and diagrams described below apply to version 1.3 and later. Earlier versions retain their existing local draft recovery.

MD Reader — Privacy Policy Effective date: 9 October 2026 MD Reader reads and edits documents on your Mac. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics or telemetry. The app does not send document text or recovery drafts to a developer-operated server. Documents and images The app accesses Markdown files you select or open through Finder. Local images require a folder you choose. Image paths must remain inside that folder. File and folder access bookmarks are stored locally to restore open tabs. Closing a tab releases its access and removes its bookmarks from the next session checkpoint. Saving a file in a cloud-synchronized folder may cause your storage provider to synchronize it under that provider's policies. Remote images Remote images are loaded by default. Their host can receive your IP address and the time of the request. You can disable them in View > Load Remote Images. This setting affects future rendering and requests; it cannot revoke requests already sent. External links open only when you click them, through your default browser or mail application. Other local files are revealed in Finder. Local recovery and preferences Unsaved edits are copied locally to Application Support/MDReader/Drafts in the app's sandbox after a short delay. Copies have private file permissions. They are removed when you save or close the document and accept discarding changes. After an interrupted session the app offers Recover, Later or Discard Copies. Recovery itself does not overwrite the original file. The current session is stored locally in Application Support/MDReader/Workspace.json in the sandbox, including the full contents of open documents (saved and unsaved), tab order, active tab, mode, cursor/scroll positions, file/folder access bookmarks and saved-content fingerprints. Quitting retains this session for the next launch; explicitly closing a tab removes it from the next checkpoint. An unreadable checkpoint is backed up locally rather than discarded. Appearance, language, zoom, remote-image preferences and up to 30 recent-file entries are stored on your Mac. Recent entries include file names/paths, opening times and file access bookmarks. Clear History or remove an entry to erase them. Recovery records can contain a document name, its original location, an access bookmark and a fingerprint of its last saved contents. Formula, code and diagram rendering use bundled libraries and do not upload your content. PDF export writes to the destination you select. Retention and control You can delete recovery copies with Discard Copies in the recovery prompt. Close tabs to remove them from the session and use Clear History for recent files. Clear sandbox application data to also remove checkpoint backups and reset local settings. The developer receives no app-collected personal data to delete.
