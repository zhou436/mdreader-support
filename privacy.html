Privacy policy

MD Reader — Privacy Policy Effective date: 2 October 2026 MD Reader reads and edits documents on your Mac. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics or telemetry. The app does not send document text or recovery drafts to a developer-operated server. Documents and images The app accesses Markdown files you select or open through Finder. Local images require a folder you choose. Image paths must remain inside that folder. Folder grants currently last for the open document's session. Saving a file in a cloud-synchronized folder may cause your storage provider to synchronize it under that provider's policies. Remote images Remote images are loaded by default. Their host can receive your IP address and the time of the request. You can disable them in View > Load Remote Images. This setting affects future rendering and requests; it cannot revoke requests already sent. External links open only when you click them, through your default browser or mail application. Other local files are revealed in Finder. Local recovery and preferences Unsaved edits are copied locally to Application Support/MDReader/Drafts in the app's sandbox after a short delay. Copies have private file permissions. They are removed when you save or close the document and accept discarding changes. After an interrupted session the app offers Recover, Later or Discard Copies. Recovery itself does not overwrite the original file. Appearance, zoom and remote-image preferences are stored on your Mac. Recovery records can contain a document name, its original location, an access bookmark and a fingerprint of its last saved contents. Retention and control You can delete recovery copies with Discard Copies in the recovery prompt. Clear stored preferences or sandbox application data to reset local settings. The developer receives no app-collected personal data to delete. Network requests made by third-party image hosts or cloud providers are subject to their own policies. 本应用在 Mac 本地读取和编辑文件，没有账户、广告、统计或遥测，不向开发者服务器上传文档或恢复草稿。远程图片默认加载，图片网站可能获得 IP 地址和请求时间；可在 View > Load Remote Images 关闭。未保存的修改有本地恢复副本，恢复前会询问用户，原文件只在明确保存时改变。云盘文件的同步由相应云盘服务管理。 Support and privacy questions https://zhou436.github.io/mdreader-support/ The support tracker is hosted on GitHub.
